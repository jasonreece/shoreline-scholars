*Explore. Learn. Play.*

Our December field trip takes students to the **Sebastian Fishing Museum**, tucked inside Sebastian Inlet State Park where the lagoon meets the Atlantic. Students explore the museum at their own pace, discovering how pioneer families built a life around the water — and then we spend the rest of the afternoon together by the inlet.

## Key Exhibits & Highlights

- **Fish House & Dock Replica** — an interior recreation honoring early local commercial fishing operations
- **Commemorated Families** — dedicated to three pioneer Sebastian fishing families: the Semblers, Smiths, and Judahs
- **Historic Gear & Boats** — a homemade fishing boat, vintage nets, antique fishing gear, and historical lagoon photographs
- **Video Presentation** — a short educational film exploring the history of the inlet and the wildlife diversity of the Indian River Lagoon
- **Gift Shop** — a small on-site shop offering local trinkets and souvenirs

## After the Museum

We will set up a tent on the north side of the inlet for the rest of the afternoon — play date! Bring lunch, chairs, and whatever your family needs for a relaxed afternoon by the water.
