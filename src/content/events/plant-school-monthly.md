*Grow. Taste. Learn.*

**Plant School** is an immersive, hands-on learning experience that teaches students how to grow, care for, use, and enjoy the food plants that thrive in our coastal Central Florida climate. Led by **Ben Thacker of Dig In Brevard**, this five-class series meets one morning a month at The Yoga Garden, where every visit centers on a new plant and a new idea.

## Students Will

- **Explore a plant of the day** — get to know one food plant up close
- **Learn a key gardening and botanical concept** — a new science topic each month
- **Touch, taste, and observe** — plants in the garden, not just on the page
- **Make a propagation** — something to take home and grow
- **Reinforce learning** — through hands-on activities and forage walks

## Monthly Class Outline

| Month | Plant of the Day | Science Topic |
|-------|------------------|---------------|
| January | Pigeon Pea / Cuban Oregano | Propagation |
| February | Lemongrass | Soil |
| March | Papaya | Pollinators |
| April | Mulberry | Composting / Worms |
| May | Sugar Cane | Seeds / Seed Saving |
