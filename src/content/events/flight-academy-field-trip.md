*Explore. Learn. Get inspired.*

Our October field trip takes students behind the scenes at EAA 724 in Merritt Island — a working aviation community where young aviators can walk the hangar, see real aircraft up close, and sit down for a classroom seminar with the people who fly them.

For our Flight Academy students, it's a wonderful way to begin the Flight Introduction Course. And every Shoreline Scholars student is welcome to come along for the adventure.

## What to Expect

- **Hangar Tour** — explore the facilities and get a behind-the-scenes look
- **View Various Aircraft** — see a variety of planes up close and learn about aviation
- **Meet with Pilots & the Chief Flight Instructor** — ask questions and learn from experienced aviators
- **Water & Light Snacks** — provided for all participants
