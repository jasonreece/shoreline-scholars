*Shop. Support. Celebrate.*

Join us for a morning of joy, creativity, and community! The Shoreline Scholars Christmas Market brings together booths, holiday shopping, and live entertainment — a Christmas showcase for our community, all under one festive roof.

Come shop small, support local, and celebrate the season with us.

## Sign Up to Be a Vendor!

Sell your products created in Entrepreneurship Class! Vendor booths are open to **student entrepreneurs and local makers** — a wonderful chance for young business owners to bring their work to a real market, and for our community's makers to share what they create.

## Sign Up to Be a Presenter!

Share your talent on stage! Music, singing, stand-up comedy, skits, spoken word, and more — **all ages welcome**. The stage is yours.
