*Join us for a day of discovery, nature, and learning!*

Our September field trip takes students to the **Sea Turtle Academy** — a hands-on study trip to explore, observe, and connect with Florida's unique coastal ecosystem. Guided by **Naturalist Mairi Brooks**, students will spend the morning at the Barrier Island Center's Doc Earhart Sanctuary, where the dunes, the shoreline, and the sea turtles themselves become the classroom.

## What to Expect

- **Wildlife Observation** — binoculars up for the creatures of the coast
- **Coastal Discovery** — explore Florida's barrier island ecosystem up close
- **Hands-On Learning** — a true study trip, guided by a naturalist
- **Friends & Community** — learning is better together
- **Making Memories** — a morning students will carry with them
