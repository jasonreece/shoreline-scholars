*Personalized support. Stronger understanding. Small groups. Big impact.*

Group Tutoring at Shoreline Scholars gives students focused, personalized help with Math & Science in a small-group setting — a maximum of five students per group, so every learner gets real attention. Whether your student needs help getting unstuck, wants to strengthen their foundation, or is ready to be challenged, these sessions meet them right where they are.

Students bring their own curriculum, and Nicole works alongside them — connecting concepts to the real world, building confidence, and supporting steady academic growth week after week.
