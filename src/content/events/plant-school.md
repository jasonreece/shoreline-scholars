*Explore. Learn. Grow a greener tomorrow.*

Our November field trip is **Plant School** — a hands-on morning in the garden with **Ben Thacker from Dig in Brevard**. Students tour The Yoga Garden, dig into the world of edible plants, and discover how small plants hold big possibilities: for our tables, our yards, and our community.

## What Students Will Experience

- **Garden Tour** — explore The Yoga Garden with an expert guide
- **Edible Plants** — learn all about the plants we can grow and eat
- **Food Forest Community** — inspiration for families to build their own
- **Propagation Activities** — students pot up plants to take home
